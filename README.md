### About Me

I am a life science researcher passionate about the intersection of **Synthetic Biology**, **Protein Language Models**, and **AI for Science**. My work spans computational chemistry, bioinformatics, and deep learning — building tools that bridge molecular understanding with data-driven discovery.

- B.S. in **Biological Science** from [Xinjiang University](https://www.xju.edu.cn/) (Top 1%)
- Summer programs at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University** (AI for Bioengineering) and **Peking University** (Quantitative Biology)
- Active in open-source: creator of [CYPForge](https://github.com/ZiyanZhuang/CYPForge), [AutoGraphRAG](https://github.com/ZiyanZhuang/AutoGraphRAG), and more
- Seeking collaborations in **AI for Science**, **CS × Biology**, and **Quantum Mechanics**
